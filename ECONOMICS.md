## What Makes This Profitable at Launch

The tool itself costs almost nothing to run:

- Vercel hosting: $0 (free tier handles thousands of requests/month)
- Supabase: $0 (free tier up to 500MB)
- Groq API: $0 (free tier sufficient for summary generation)
- Resend email: $0 (free tier up to 3,000 emails/month)
- Domain: ~$10/year

**Total infrastructure cost: ~$10/month for first 6 months**

The tool is profitable from the **first customer conversion** since CAC is
near zero and infrastructure cost is negligible.

## Path to $1M ARR in 18 Months

For $1M ARR at $3,000 per customer: need **333 paying customers**

Month-by-month path:

- Month 1–3: 10 customers (organic launch, HN, ProductHunt)
- Month 4–6: 30 customers (word of mouth + Credex customer base email)
- Month 7–12: 100 customers (SEO kicks in, referral loop from shareable URLs)
- Month 13–18: 333 customers (paid acquisition now justified at known LTV:CAC)

**What must be true for this to work:**

1. Audit-to-email conversion stays above 20% — requires the results page to
   genuinely show value, not manufactured savings
2. Email-to-consultation conversion reaches 15% — requires a good follow-up
   sequence from Credex sales team
3. At least one high-distribution moment in month 1 (HN front page OR a
   tweet from someone with 10k+ followers)
4. Credex's discounted credits are actually cheaper than retail — the tool
   only generates leads if the underlying product delivers on the promise

The biggest risk is not distribution — it is that users complete the audit,
see savings opportunities, but switch directly to cheaper retail plans rather
than booking a Credex consultation. Mitigation: surface Credex prominently
for high-savings cases and make the consultation CTA frictionless (one click,
not a form).
