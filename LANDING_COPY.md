## FAQ

**Q: Is this actually free?**
A: Yes. No account needed. No credit card. You see your results immediately
and only share your email if you want to save the report.

**Q: How accurate is the pricing data?**
A: Every price is pulled from official vendor pricing pages and verified
during submission week. We cite the source URL for every number in our
PRICING_DATA file. Prices change — we note the verification date so you
know how fresh the data is.

**Q: Do you store my data?**
A: If you enter your email, we store your audit result and email in Supabase
to send you the report. If you don't enter your email, nothing is stored
beyond the anonymous audit result needed to power the shareable URL.

**Q: What AI tools do you cover?**
A: Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Claude, ChatGPT, OpenAI API, Anthropic API,
Gemini, and Windsurf. These cover the vast majority of AI spend for
engineering and writing teams.

**Q: What if the audit says I'm already spending optimally?**
A: We tell you that honestly. "You're spending well" is a valid audit result.
We don't manufacture savings that aren't there. You can still sign up to be
notified when better options become available for your stack.
